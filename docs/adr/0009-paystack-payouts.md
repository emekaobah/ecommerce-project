# 9. Paying shops with Paystack Transfers

Status: accepted, 2026-10-09 (#29)

## Context

Shops are paid after delivery, less a flat 10% commission, by Paystack Transfer. Oja absorbs Paystack's fees. A shop's order in a split checkout is one share of one Paystack payment (#20), and it can be refunded in part: when items are unavailable (#22), by staff, or by hand in Paystack's dashboard. Paystack takes refunds out of the same balance payouts are paid from.

Facts that shaped the decision (checked 2026-10-09):

- Mercur 2.3.5's payout module takes exactly one provider (`IPayoutProvider`). With none configured it registers a system provider that marks every payout paid without paying anyone.
- Mercur's `createPayoutWorkflow` pays `order.total` less the order's commission lines. That total includes the delivery fee, and a refund doesn't change it, so it would pay a shop for refunded goods. The workflow doesn't link the payout to its order, and it passes the order id to the provider as an idempotency key, but nothing stops a second payout for the same order.
- Mercur's `payout` table only accepts `pending`, `paid`, `failed` and `canceled` as statuses. Its `PayoutStatus` enum also has `processing`, and an insert with that status is refused.
- Mercur's module creates a payout only after the provider has made the transfer. So a transfer's reference can't be the payout's id.
- A Paystack account sends every webhook to one URL. We already point it at the payment webhook (`/hooks/payment/paystack_paystack`), so `transfer.*` events arrive there and never at Mercur's `/hooks/payout`.
- Courier labels are paid from Oja's Shipbubble wallet (#24), so the delivery fee the buyer paid is Oja's money.
- An order edit (#22) lowers `order.total`. It doesn't change what was captured for the order, only what is refunded. Until the refund is made, the order owes the buyer the difference: Medusa's order summary has a negative `pending_difference`.

## Decision

- **Our own payout provider** (`src/modules/paystack-payout`), registered on Mercur's payout module when `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` is set. It has one Paystack account per currency (`accounts.ngn` for now), which is how a seller in another country will be paid. Onboarding is Oja's Shop setup page (#26), so a payout account is active from the start and holds nothing. Each payout names its recipient, read from the shop's bank account when the payout is made. It is never read from the payout account, which members can create through Mercur's route. A queued transfer is `pending`, `success` is `paid`, and `failed`, `reversed` (and Paystack's other end states) are `failed`. A payout of nothing sends no transfer and is `paid`.
- **Our own payout workflow** (`payOutOrderWorkflow`) in place of Mercur's. The amount is worked out explicitly (`payoutOf`): what was captured for the order, less its refunds of items and what it still owes the buyer, less the delivery fee, less 10% of what is left. The calculation runs in kobo. The order's refund lock is held while it runs, so a refund and a payout of the same order never interleave.
- **One `shop_payout` row per attempt at an order** (in the order-fulfilment module), written before Paystack is asked to transfer. `order_id` with `attempt`, and `transfer_reference`, are unique, and an order with a live or paid attempt isn't tried again, so an order is paid once and Paystack won't make the same transfer twice. The row keeps how the payout was worked out. The transfer and its status stay in Mercur's payout, linked to the order and the seller. If Paystack refuses the transfer, the row is removed and the order is tried again on the next run. A transfer that fails later (`transfer.failed` or `reversed`) keeps its row and is retried on the next run as a new attempt (`attempt`, reference `payout_<order id>_<n>`), up to three attempts. After the third failure the admin is notified to pay the shop by hand (`nextPayoutAttempt`). If Paystack took the transfer and something after it failed, the row is kept and the error is logged.
- **The daily `pay-out-delivered-orders` job** (01:00 UTC) pays each order whose `payout_eligible_at` is set. Delivered sets it (#25), and Collected will too (#70). It skips orders already paid out and orders with a refund that is still `pending`, `processing` or `needs-attention`. A shop's orders are paid one at a time, oldest first.
- **A refund after a payout is taken from the shop's next payouts.** What a shop owes back is what its paid-out orders were paid beyond what they are owed now, less what later payouts already took back (`owedBack`). Only orders that have had a refund are worked out again, and payouts that failed count for nothing. A delivered order cancelled since is never paid. If a refund that was taken back later fails, the shop owes less than nothing, and the next payout gives the difference back.
- **`transfer.*` webhooks** are handled with the payment webhooks (`src/subscribers/paystack-webhook.ts`). The signature is checked by the route, and the transfer's status is fetched from Paystack before Mercur's `processPayoutForWebhookWorkflow` sets the payout paid or failed.
- **The daily `check-paystack-refunds` job** (00:30 UTC, before the payouts) lists the previous UTC day's Paystack refunds (`GET /refund`). Any refund whose id no `order_refund` row records is logged and posted to the admin's notifications.
- **Commission shown in the panels**: the seed sets Mercur's global commission rate to 10%, tax included, delivery left out, so Mercur's commission line on each order matches the commission in our payout as the order was placed. Only the admin panel shows Mercur's commission lines now; the vendor panel shows only the payout's commission (#23).

## Consequences

- The vendor panel's Payouts page (Mercur's) lists a shop's payouts. Each order page shows its payout through our `GET /vendor/orders/:id/payout`: what the buyer paid, delivery, refunds, commission, the payout, anything taken back, and where it is. Like `/vendor/payouts`, it needs `payout:read`.
- Mercur's commission lines don't follow refunds made without an order edit, and payouts don't read them. So shops never see them (#23): `GET /vendor/orders/:id/commission-lines` is refused (403), Mercur's order summary then leaves its commission line out, and the payout's commission, which is what the shop is charged, is the only one on the order page. The 403 is deliberate: Mercur's panel extensions can add to the order page but can't remove the summary's commission row, and refusing the route is the one way to keep it off without forking the page. A 403 also keeps the lines from shops that call the API directly.
- A refund records what it covers: items, delivery, or all of a share nobody is paid for (a rejected order's, or a payment no order claims) (#22). Shops refund only items, so the vendor refund route always records items, at most what was captured less the delivery fee and the earlier refunds of items that haven't failed; with no amount it refunds all of that, never the delivery fee. A refund of items is taken off what the shop is paid for goods. Only Oja's staff refund delivery (`POST /admin/orders/:id/delivery-refund`, and the admin order page's Delivery refund widget, which shows what is left of the fee), at most the delivery fee less what was refunded of it already. Both caps are checked under the order's refund lock. Oja keeps the delivery fee, so it bears a refund of it: `payoutOf` takes it off Oja's delivery share, never off the shop's payout, and the payout doesn't wait for it to settle.
- Lines an order edit removed (items unavailable at picking, #22; a pickup order's non-perishable lines once Not collected, #32) are refunded after the edit. The shop is never paid for them, however the refund goes: until refunded they are what the order owes the buyer, which `payoutOf` takes off the goods as it would their refund. So a Not collected order is paid only its perishable lines.
- **A refund Paystack refuses is owed, and retried (#16).** What an order owes stays derived from Medusa's order summary (`owedToBuyerOf`), less what Oja's team settled by hand; there is no separate ledger. Every refund of what is owed (`refundOwedWorkflow`, under the order's refund lock) is a refund of items, or, for an accepted order that was cancelled (#17), of all that is left of its share (`all`): its captures less every refund of it made (`shareLeft`), which it owes its buyer until refunded. A cancelled order is never paid out, and its payout reads ₦0: its buyer keeps none of its lines, so no Oja-funded discount is added back either. Each refused attempt is an `order_refund` row that is `failed` from the start, with Paystack's reason (`failure_reason`) and no Medusa refund; the attempts are the failed refunds of items since one last went through or Oja's team last settled what was owed by hand, so a refund Paystack accepted and failed later counts too, and a later edit owing more starts a fresh backoff. Only Paystack refusing a payment is an attempt, recorded for what was left unrefunded: a failure after Paystack made the refund (recording it on the order, say) fails the refund without one, and a refund Paystack made that the order doesn't record isn't owed, so it is never made twice. The `retry-owed-refunds` job runs every 5 minutes and retries an owed refund 5 minutes, an hour and 6 hours after each refusal, skipping an order while a refund of it is `pending`, `processing` or `needs-attention`. The edit's own refund is the first attempt, so after the third retry fails (four attempts) the refund goes to manual handling. An amount under Paystack's ₦50 minimum goes there at once, and Paystack is never asked. Manual handling posts to the admin's notifications and tells the buyer by push and email that "our team is sending your ₦X refund", once per order and amount: the admin's notice is the record that it was handed over, so later runs of the job leave it be.
- **Oja's staff handle owed refunds in the admin's Owed refunds list** (Orders → Owed refunds; `GET /admin/owed-refunds`): order, buyer, amount, attempts and Paystack's last error. "Retry now" (`POST /admin/owed-refunds/:id/retry`) tries it whatever its backoff, even in manual handling, but not while a refund of the order is still in Paystack's hands (`409`). "Mark settled" (`POST /admin/owed-refunds/:id/settle`) records a `refund_settlement`: the amount, how it was sent (bank transfer or other) and a note. A settled amount is no longer retried or listed. The order summary still owes it, so it still comes off the shop's payout: the buyer was paid back from Oja's money, which the shop is never paid.
- **The buyer sees what is owed**: the order's `owed_refund` reads "Refund of ₦X pending" while it is retried and "Our team is sending your ₦X refund" in manual handling. A refused attempt was never made, so it isn't shown as a refund that failed.
- Paying one transfer per order costs a Paystack transfer fee per order.
- A discount lowers what was captured, so the shop bears it, unless Oja funds it: ADR 0010 (#6) adds those back.
